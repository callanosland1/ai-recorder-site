Privacy policy

AI Recorder · last updated 27 November 2026

What the app collects

Audio you choose to record, the transcripts made from it on your device, and the names, folders and speaker labels you give them. Nothing is sent to the developer or to any analytics, advertising or tracking service.

Where your data is kept

  • On your iPhone, in the app's own storage.
  • On your own Mac, if you pair it, sent directly over your private network.
  • In your own Google Drive, if you turn on the cloud library.

Google Drive

When you sign in with Google, the app asks only for the drive.file permission. That lets it see and change only the files and folders it creates itself (an "AI Recorder Library" folder and the transcript documents in it). It cannot see any other file in your Drive. It uses this access only to create, update, rename, move and organise those transcript documents. Data from Google is not shared with anyone, sold, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.

The sign-in token is kept in your iPhone's keychain. Signing out in the app revokes it. You can also remove the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Files already in your Drive stay there until you delete them.

Voice recognition

If you name speakers, the app keeps voice profiles on your own devices only, so it can recognise them again. "Forget this person" removes a profile.

Assistants

If you connect an AI assistant (for example Claude) to your Mac, it can read and organise your recordings only after you approve it on your Mac, and it can never delete them.

Deleting your data

Delete recordings in the app, delete the "AI Recorder Library" folder in Google Drive, or remove the app. Nothing is kept anywhere else.
