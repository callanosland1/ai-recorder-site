AI Recorder

A personal voice recorder for iPhone and Mac.

AI Recorder records audio on your iPhone, transcribes it on the device, and keeps your recordings in sync between your iPhone and your own Mac. If you choose, it also saves a text copy of each transcript to a folder in your own Google Drive, so you can read it there or ask an assistant about it.

It is a personal app used by its developer. It is not sold and has no accounts, ads or analytics.
